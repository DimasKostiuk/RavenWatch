# Changelog

## 2.8.0

**English**

- The feeder is not a chest: a raven at home throws out of it whatever is not food.
- The raven's pouch does not open while the raven is away: it is on its back.
- The gifts of both ravens no longer show as Hugin's own in the book, and with one raven on you they are not listed at all.
- «Double Load»: the ravens also soften your falls from a height, even more with its third golden rune.
- A gift ends when its raven is taken off or flies away, instead of going on without it.
- A stranger's raven no longer gives you its gifts.
- The paired gifts no longer take strength from one raven when the other has too little.
- New golden errand «Hugin's Trail to the Hoard»: follow the raven from sign to sign to a hidden hoard, and face its keeper.
- New golden errand «Siege of the Nest»: hold your nest through three night waves of beasts and their leader.
- «Odin's Riddle» has many more riddles, now for every land up to the Deep North.
- Under sail the ravens sometimes play behind the ship as a pair, your own two or with a friend raven of another player.
- In Hugin's shadow the others no longer see you by anything: no dust or sound from your steps, no light, wisp, flying ravens or the looks of your gifts around you.
- R at a perch no longer now and then hides or takes out your weapons instead of leaving the raven there.
- A raven taken from a perch while it is already with you no longer crumbles into feathers with a wrong message: the perch just frees its seat.
- Odin's errand to hang trophies now wants fresh ones: hunt a beast, take its trophy and hang it on any stand (the old trophy wall hung again counts nothing).
- A boss beaten with your raven counts for its strike again even if you died in the fight: the raven only has to be on you when the boss appears and again when it falls (back from your grave too).
- The ravens' voices fit the moment: quiet when you sneak or hide in the shadow, louder in a fight or a thunderstorm, slower when the raven droops, a little tipsy after your mead, with an echo in caves and dungeons; now and then a caw after a line.
- With RavenWatch_Voice 1.2.0 each raven gets one of twice as many voices and keeps it for good, and the game's big Munin speaks too.
- Each raven speaks in its own voice, matching its caw (higher or lower, hoarse, muffled or thin), the beak moves with every syllable, a raven far from you calls out louder, and while one raven speaks the other now and then nods or croaks along.
- Now and then one raven breaks into the other's long line: the first stops mid-word, the other cuts in (Munin does it more often).
- Ravens learn words: from trust 3 a raven asks you to teach it a word, now and then picks one up from the chat, and drops them into its talk, mangled at first.
- Seal ᚨ beside the raven's name in the book: its words, how clean it says them and when to say each one; changing a word's moment or forgetting a word costs a sparklet.
- Mead: when you drink, a raven now and then takes a sip and is tipsy for a minute: flies crooked, hiccups and talks drunk nonsense.
- New Munin passive «Blood Revenge»: he marks your killer; kill it soon after you are back up and he returns the skills the death took.
- New Hugin passive «Omen»: some mornings he foretells the day's raid and weather — turn the raid aside or call it now; also bad omens and warnings at sea.
- New Munin gift «Taming»: he lands on a wild beast's head and breaks it in — while he sits it tames faster and attacks nobody.
- Rare ravens now have a trait of their own: each breed helps in its own way (see the knack card in the book) and has its own words.
- Odin's feather has its own icon and is black and gold with a faint glow.
- By deep water or on a ship a raven dives for a fish: a hungry Munin eats it, otherwise it brings it to you.
- Put on a thing you never wore before, and a raven looks you over and comments (not on every change).
- Ravens pick up habits from how you play — mead, home or the road, fights, food, petting, night or day, the sea, dungeons: each with a plus and a minus, shown in the book.
- Now and then a raven flies off to wash at the water's edge, sooner when it got dirty, and shakes the water off onto you.
- A raven plays tag with your tamed beast, by itself or on your word: look at the beast, and the «Perch» button in the orders circle becomes «Play».
- A grazing deer or any meadow beast near you no longer counts as a fight for the ravens: their visits and moments go on; a boar or a neck coming at you still does.
- The fight judge: after a fight worth it the raven on your shoulder rates it from one to five stars and has a word on it; the best fight is remembered.
- The rare raven «Odd-Eyed» is now «Golden-Eyed»: both its eyes burn with gold.
- A raven asked for a gift while dozing wakes up for it.
- Odin's fishing errands come only once you have a rod, «The Old Crypt» no longer sends you to a sunken crypt before the Elder, and a few errands got truer texts and lists.
- A raven's wishes depend more on its character and habits, and it rarely asks for the same thing twice in a row; a devoted raven asks more often, a fidget less often but for longer adventures.
- The ravens' strength under their effect icons reads again without SeneaL UI.
- The Raven's Nest looks sharper: its textures and glowing runes are in full quality.
- The gift cards in the book are full size again with many gifts.
- The words page shows its arrow and cross instead of empty squares.
- One monster lured by a raven and scared by another player's raven no longer jerks between the two.
- A monster another player's Hugin already teases cannot be teased again.
- A monster caught where two players' domes overlap is pushed out instead of standing inside both.
- «Blood Revenge»: a killer of two players avenges both when it dies.
- Odin's kill errands count a kill for everyone who has one and dealt a good share of the damage to the beast, not only for the one who finished it.
- A raven that comes down beside you while you sit or lie no longer walks in the air past a ledge or behind a wall: it only lands on a real floor next to you.
- A raven that gets up from beside you or off your chest by itself no longer flies on with its legs down as if still standing.
