# RavenWatch — English glossary and style

Every term below is used the same way in the menus (ui.txt) and in the ravens' words (phrase files).
Status: DRAFT, to be approved by the author.

## Style

- American English (color, armor, gray) — as Valheim itself.
- The player is "you", informal. The ravens never say "the player".
- **Hugin** — dry, clever, a little smug; teases, never curses hard ("idiot", "fool", "for Odin's sake").
- **Munin** — crude and loud; swears as much as in Ukrainian (fuck, shit, damn, bastard, ass); fond of you underneath.
  No slurs about orientation, nationality, race — the same rule as in Ukrainian.
- Trust tone: `~1` cold and distant, `~2` warmer, `~3` close (inside jokes, "old friend").
- Not word for word: puns, rhymes and swears are rewritten so they work in English.
- Raven sounds: "Кар!" → "Caw!", "Кра." → "Kraa.".
- Quotes: Ukrainian «…» → English “…” (in the menus and in speech).
- Numbers: 1.5 (decimal point), units m, s, km, min.
- Names of Valheim things (bosses, biomes, items, creatures): exactly as the English game has them.

## Creature names in the lines

English has no cases, so the forms of creature_names.txt mean: `{x}` the bare name (troll, Eikthyr), `{x_gen}` / `{x_ins}` the name with its article (the troll, Eikthyr, the Elder), `{x_genpl}` the plural (trolls). Write `{killer_gen}` where English needs “the …”, and `{boss_gen}` for bosses.
